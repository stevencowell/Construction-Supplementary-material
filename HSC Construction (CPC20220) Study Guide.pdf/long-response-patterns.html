HSC VET Construction: Section III Game Plan

1. Where Section III Fits

Section III is the major extended response part of the HSC exam. The Canva guide frames it as a game plan: do not just start writing. Break the question down, plan the answer, then write with control.

SectionRecommended timeMain job
Section I10 minutesMove quickly through multiple choice and avoid overthinking.
Section II20 minutesAnswer short responses clearly with correct construction detail.
Section III60 minutesPlan and write the extended response with examples, judgement and links back to the question.

2. The Three-Step Game Plan

  1. Deconstruct: find the verb, the topic, the depth words and the scenario.
  2. Plan: choose 4-5 strong points before writing. Each point needs a construction example and a clear effect.
  3. Communicate: write in paragraphs that answer the question directly, not in loose notes or repeated statements.

3. Deconstruct The Question

The command verb is only the starting point. The depth words tell students how far to go.

  • Verb: describe, explain, assess, justify or evaluate.
  • Depth: benefits, drawbacks, impacts, strategies, examples, judgement or consequences.
  • Context: workers, employers, clients, trades, equipment, site safety, environment or communication.

Good exam habit: write a short version of the question in your own words before planning.

4. Plan With A TEEL-Style Structure

The Canva guide uses TEEL thinking: topic sentence, explanation, evidence/example and link. For Construction, add positives, negatives and judgement when the question asks for depth.

PartWhat students should do
TopicState the main point for the paragraph and make sure it answers the question.
ExplainExplain how or why the point matters in a construction workplace.
ExampleUse a specific site example, such as PPE, SWMS, manual handling, dust, tool checks, trade coordination or erosion control.
AdvantagesShow the positive outcome: safer work, better quality, less waste, improved communication, lower cost or fewer delays.
DisadvantagesWhen relevant, identify the drawback, challenge, risk or limitation.
Link / JudgementLink back to the question and make a judgement about why the point is important.

5. Strong Paragraph Pattern

Weak: Workers should wear PPE because it keeps them safe.

Stronger: Workers cutting timber should wear eye protection, hearing protection and a dust mask because the task creates flying particles, noise and fine dust. This reduces the chance of eye injury, hearing damage and breathing problems. However, PPE should not be the only control because it does not remove the hazard. A better response also links PPE to higher-level controls, such as dust extraction, guards, safe work procedures and supervision.

6. What Repeats Across 2021-2025

  • Safety and risk control: hazards, controls, procedures, PPE, manual handling and safe work practices.
  • Workplace systems: communication, teamwork, sequencing, conflict and coordination between trades.
  • Tools and equipment: selection, use, maintenance, checking, faults and safe operation.
  • Environment and resources: erosion, waste, sustainability, resource efficiency and reducing site impact.

7. Quick Exam Routine

  • Circle the command verb.
  • Underline the construction topic.
  • Box the depth words.
  • Identify the scenario and who is involved.
  • Plan 4-5 paragraphs using topic, explain, example, positives, negatives and link / judgement.

Bottom Line

Section III is not a memory dump. The best answers deconstruct the question, plan before writing, and communicate using construction examples, clear effects and judgement.
