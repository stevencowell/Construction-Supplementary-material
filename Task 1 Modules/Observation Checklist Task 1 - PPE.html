Task 1: PPE Observation Checklist

For this task, you must demonstrate to your teacher that you can correctly select, fit, and adjust all the mandatory PPE. Check off each item below as you practise.

💡 Tutor's Fitting Tips

Hard Hat: Adjust the internal harness so it's snug on your head. It shouldn't be loose or fall off when you bend over.

Eye Protection: Safety glasses should fit closely to your face, protecting you from the front and sides.

Hearing Protection: Earmuffs must form a complete seal around your ears. For earplugs, roll them tight and insert them fully.

Hi-Vis Clothing: This must be your outermost layer of clothing to ensure you are always visible to others.

All items checked! You're ready for your observation.
